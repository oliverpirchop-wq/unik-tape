The company

From trader to manufacturer.

In 1993 Xhelal Neziri started trading adhesive tape from Slovenia. We have been manufacturing ourselves since 2008, and since 2016 as UNIK TAPE with our own machinery in Fushë Kosovë.

Today we supply more than 200 clients with packing, masking, cloth and printed tape, under your own brand if you wish.

Range

Seven tape families for industry and trade.

Packing tape

BOPP packing tapes with acrylic, hot-melt or natural rubber adhesive, for hand and machine application.

Printed tape

Packing tape with your logo: branding and tamper evidence on every carton.

Masking tape

Masking tapes from 60 °C to 80 °C for painters, refinishers and industry.

Cloth tape

Strong cloth duct tape for sealing, bundling and fixing.

Mesh tape

Mesh tape from our own production. Technical data to follow.

Photoluminescent safety tape

Charges in light and glows in the dark: for escape routes, steps and edges.

PVC & protective tape

PVC tapes for pipe wrapping, surface protection and packaging.
