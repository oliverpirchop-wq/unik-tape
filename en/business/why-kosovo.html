Business Services/Why Kosovo?

Why Kosovo?

Close to DACH. In language, time and working style.

Prishtina is roughly a two-hour flight from the major German-speaking cities and in the same time zone.

German is widely spoken

Many people in Kosovo have lived in Germany, Austria or Switzerland or have family there. Our team works at C1 level.

Same time zone

Central European Time: your team is available during your office hours.

Euro as currency

The euro is used in Kosovo. You calculate without exchange-rate risk.

Young, well-educated talent

Kosovo has one of the youngest populations in Europe and many motivated graduates.

Economical hourly rates

€15–35 per hour with personal support from a dedicated team.

Stable infrastructure

Two separate internet lines and a UPS keep the office working even during outages.

To be frank

Kosovo is not an EU member. For data protection this means cooperation needs a sound contractual basis. How we handle this is explained under “Data protection & quality”.
