Business Services/FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what prospects want to know first.

What does cooperation cost?

Between €15 and €35 per hour, depending on the service. For enterprise clients we prepare a custom quote.

How do we start?

With a free 30-minute call. This is followed by a 5-hour trial project at €15 per hour, then the quote and a monthly contract.

Which languages does the team speak?

German at C1 level and English.

When is the team available?

We work in Central European Time (CET), i.e. during your office hours. Exact service hours are agreed in the quote.

How long am I committed?

Cooperation runs on a monthly contract. Notice period: to be confirmed.

Can the team grow or shrink?

Yes. Teams are scalable and are adjusted to your needs.

Who is my contact?

You get personal team management: one dedicated person who leads your team and reports to you.

What happens in a power or internet outage?

The office has two internet lines and an uninterruptible power supply (UPS).

What about data protection?

Under the GDPR, Kosovo is a third country. The contractual documents are being prepared; see “Data protection & quality” for details.

Which industries do you work for?

E-commerce, real estate, logistics, insurance, tax firms, and IT and SaaS.
