About us

Always in pursuit of innovation.

UNIK TAPE & Tech LLC is a company from Fushë Kosovë that has worked with adhesive tape for more than 30 years: first as a trader, today as a manufacturer.

Who we are

Manufacturer and distributor of adhesive tape

We produce packing, masking and cloth tape, print tape for private labels and distribute adhesive products in Kosovo and abroad.

1993founded
200+clients

Our story

From 1993 to today

  1. 1993

    The beginning

    Xhelal Neziri founds a trading company for imported goods from AERO in Celje, Slovenia. The first product: Aero Tape.

  2. 2008

    Own production

    Local production of packing and masking tape begins.

  3. 2016

    UNIK TAPE is born

    The company UNIK TAPE is founded with a new production line.

  4. 2018

    Speciality tapes

    Expansion into printed tape, masking tapes for higher temperatures, cloth and mesh tape.

  5. Today

    More than 200 clients

    We supply clients in Kosovo and abroad and are expanding sales in Europe.

What we do

Three pillars

Manufacturing

Packing, masking, cloth and speciality tapes from our own production.

Private Label

Printed tape and adhesive products under the customer’s brand.

Distribution & trade

Supply across Kosovo and abroad.
