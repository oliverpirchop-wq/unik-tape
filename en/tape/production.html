Production & delivery

Made in Fushë Kosovë. Delivered in Europe.

We have been manufacturing ourselves since 2008. A new production line followed in 2016, speciality tapes in 2018.

Manufacturing

From slitting the jumbo roll to the finished roll

We slit, rewind, print and pack adhesive tape at our own site. This lets us supply widths from 18 to 150 mm and lengths from 30 to 990 m, as hand or machine rolls.

  • Packing, masking and cloth tape
  • Printed tape and private label
  • Bulk and custom orders
